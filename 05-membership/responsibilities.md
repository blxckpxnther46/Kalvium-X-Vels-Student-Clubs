# Member Responsibilities

Active membership in the Kalvium × Vels Student Clubs carries specific expectations.

---

## 📜 Standard Code of Member Conduct

1. **Active Participation**: Attend scheduled Growth Hours sessions punctually and engage in team activities.
2. **Sprint Contribution**: Complete assigned tasks towards the club's monthly showcase deliverable.
3. **Respect & Collaboration**: Maintain professional, respectful interactions with peers, coordinators, and mentors.
4. **Care for Facilities**: Treat campus labs, AV hardware, and board games with care and respect.
5. **Attendance Threshold**: Maintain a minimum 75% attendance record across scheduled growth sessions.

---

## 🛑 Inactive Member Status
Members who fail to meet attendance thresholds or abandon sprint responsibilities without prior notice will be moved to inactive status and lose eligibility for Showcase Day participation and certificates.
