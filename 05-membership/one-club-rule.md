# The One-Club-Only Policy

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Every student may be enrolled in ONLY ONE club at a time.** Simultaneous active membership across multiple clubs is strictly prohibited.

---

## 🎯 Rationale & Objectives

The One-Club-Only Policy was instituted to ensure:

1. **Meaningful Focus**: Deep engagement in one skill area yields higher quality learning than surface-level attendance in four.
2. **Project Completion**: Members commit enough time to complete tangible showcase artifacts rather than abandoning projects midway.
3. **Team Accountability**: Group sprint teams can depend on consistent attendance from every team member.
4. **Resource Optimization**: Room caps and workstation allocations remain predictable and fair for all participants.

---

## 🔄 Skill Expansion Through Cycles
This rule does **not** lock a student into one domain permanently. Students expand their skills by completing a cycle in one club and using the [Club Switching Protocol](club-switching.md) to experience another club in the next cycle.
