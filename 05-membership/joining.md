# Joining a Club

Joining a student club is open to eligible students across participating academic batches.

---

## 📝 Registration Step-by-Step

1. **Access the Central Form**: Open the official **Kalvium × Vels Student Club Enrollment Form** during published registration windows.
2. **Review Policy Acknowledgement**: Read and accept the [One-Club-Only Policy](one-club-rule.md) and Code of Conduct.
3. **Submit Details**: Enter your name, student ID, batch, and academic year accurately.
4. **Select Your Primary Club**: Choose ONE club from the available options.
5. **Indicate Leadership Interest**: Optionally fill out the leadership application section if seeking an officer role.
6. **Confirmation**: Receive automated confirmation and await your VP's welcome email with session location details.

---

## ⚠️ Important Capacity Rule
If your desired club (such as 2D & 3D Club) has reached capacity, you will be offered a waitlist position or prompted to select an alternative club for the active cycle.
