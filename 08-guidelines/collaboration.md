# Cross-Club Collaboration Rules

Joint initiatives enrich student experiences but must respect foundational ecosystem governance.

---

## 📜 Collaboration Principles

1. **Strict Single Roster Principle**: Collaborative projects do **not** permit dual membership. Every student remains accountable to their home club's attendance and VP.
2. **Equal Credit Attribution**: All published deliverables from joint projects must prominently display the names and home clubs of all contributors.
3. **Prior Leadership Sign-Off**: Joint initiatives require explicit agreement between the Presidents of all involved clubs during monthly sprint planning.
