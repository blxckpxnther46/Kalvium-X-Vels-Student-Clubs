# Code of Conduct

The Kalvium × Vels Student Clubs ecosystem is committed to providing a safe, inclusive, and professional environment for all participants.

---

## 🛡️ Core Conduct Standards

1. **Inclusivity & Respect**: Treat all members, officers, mentors, and guests with dignity regardless of background, gender, or skill level.
2. **Zero Tolerance for Harassment**: Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or abusive language in person or online will result in immediate expulsion.
3. **Academic & Creative Integrity**: Plagiarism of code, 3D assets, design work, or written speeches is strictly prohibited. Give credit where due.
4. **Professional Representation**: Maintain high ethical standards when representing the university at external events or online platforms.
5. **Responsible Asset Use**: Treat campus infrastructure, software licenses, and physical game sets with care.
