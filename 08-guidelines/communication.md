# Internal Communication Guidelines

Clear, structured communication prevents misunderstandings and keeps operations transparent.

---

## 💬 Approved Communication Channels

- **Official Google Workspace Email**: Formal announcements, official proposals, mentor updates.
- **Central Google Chat / Workspace Channels**: Session updates, team syncs, logistical notices.

---

## 📌 Chat Etiqutte Rules

1. **Use Professional Tone**: Avoid excessive spam, inappropriate memes, or off-topic arguments in official channels.
2. **Check Before Asking**: Review posted documentation and pinned messages before asking routine questions.
3. **Respect Quiet Hours**: Avoid tagging members or mentors with non-urgent requests after 8:00 PM.
