# The Monthly Showcase Day

**Showcase Day** is the monthly culmination of the ecosystem's efforts, held generally on the final weekend of the month (subject to approved campus scheduling).

---

## 🎪 Event Objectives & Scope

Showcase Day serves as a formal public platform where all four clubs present their completed sprint artifacts to the university community.

---

## 🏆 Formats per Club

- **2D & 3D Club**: Interactive art gallery, UI prototype demos, 3D asset renders, and playable 2D game booths.
- **Social Media Club**: Screening of student short films, podcast episode launches, and brand growth campaign summaries.
- **Abstract Strategies Club**: Live finals of chess/monopoly tournaments, business strategy tear-down presentations.
- **Debate & Public Speaking**: Live keynote competition, parliamentary debate showcase, and elevator pitch battles.

---

## 🌐 Public Publishing & Social Distribution
Outstanding projects from Showcase Day are curated and published on official university channels (LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube) to celebrate student achievement.
