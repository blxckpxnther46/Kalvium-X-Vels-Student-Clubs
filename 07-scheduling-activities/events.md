# Events & Workshops

Beyond regular Growth Hours, clubs may host special events, inter-college hackathons, and guest masterclasses.

---

## 📅 Event Categories

1. **Internal Masterclasses**: Specialized deep-dive workshops hosted by invited faculty or senior student experts.
2. **Inter-Club Challenges**: Joint hackathons or strategy battles combining design, strategy, and pitching.
3. **Guest Speaker Sessions**: Virtual or in-person keynotes from industry professionals in design, media, or business.

---

## 📋 Event Planning Checklist
- [ ] Submit Event Proposal to Operations Team 14 days prior.
- [ ] Secure venue and AV equipment approvals.
- [ ] Publish promotional media via Social Media Club.
- [ ] Conduct post-event feedback survey and archive recordings.
