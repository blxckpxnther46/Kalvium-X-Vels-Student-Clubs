# Growth Hours & Timings

The primary time windows allocated for student club activities are designated as **Growth Hours**.

---

## ⏰ Proposed Schedule

```
TUESDAY    : 4:00 PM – 6:00 PM  (Dedicated Club Growth Hours)
THURSDAY   : 4:00 PM – 6:00 PM  (Dedicated Club Growth Hours)
SATURDAY   : Available hours on working Saturdays (excluding academic classes)
```

---

## ⚠️ Academic Integration & Status Disclaimer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The schedules listed above are **PROPOSED** operational windows and are **NOT automatically officially approved**. 
> 
> All scheduling must account for:
> 1. Core academic lecture and laboratory timetables.
> 2. 2nd-Year course commitments and 3rd-Year Coding Hours.
> 3. Work Integration (WI) period structures (balancing work simulations versus external company internships).
> 
> Any alternative session arrangements using WI hours must be feasible, agreed upon by batch coordinators, and approved by campus management prior to implementation.
