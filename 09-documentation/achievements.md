# Achievements & Wall of Fame

The **Wall of Fame** honors individuals and club teams that demonstrate exceptional initiative, leadership, and craft.

---

## 🏆 Recognition Categories

- **Best Showcase Artifact of the Month**: Awarded to the team producing the highest quality output during Showcase Day.
- **Leadership Excellence Award**: Presented to officers who demonstrate outstanding team management and operational rigor.
- **Growth Champion**: Recognized members who exhibit remarkable skill transformation across consecutive cycles.

---

## 🌟 Public Highlight
Winners are featured on the official university website and highlighted across Social Media Club broadcasts.
