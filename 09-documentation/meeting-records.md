# Meeting Records & Attendance

Maintaining accurate meeting minutes and attendance records ensures operational transparency.

---

## 📝 Responsibilities of the Vice President

1. **Attendance Sheets**: Record member attendance digitally during every Growth Hours session.
2. **Meeting Minutes (MoM)**: Document key decisions, assigned tasks, and deadlines during executive board meetings.
3. **Central Archiving**: Store all MoM files in the designated Google Drive folder within 24 hours of meeting completion.
