# 2D & 3D Club

> **Core Action Tag**: `CREATE`  
> **Primary Focus**: Visual creativity, digital design, animation, game dev, interactive media, 3D modelling, and rendering.

---

## 🎨 Overview & Purpose

The **2D & 3D Club** serves as the creative design hub of the university ecosystem. It brings together digital artists, UI/UX designers, animators, and game developers to transform abstract concepts into striking visual assets and interactive environments.

---

## 🎯 Key Activity Streams

| Category | Specific Activities & Tools | Example Sprint Deliverables |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Traditional & Digital Art** | Painting, Drawing, Concept Art, Illustration | Character concept sheets, digital painting portfolios |
| **UI/UX & Web Design** | Wireframing, Prototyping, Figma, Design Systems | Mobile app UI kits, website interactive prototypes |
| **2D Animation & Gaming** | Vector animation, Sprite design, Unity 2D, Godot | 2D indie game prototypes, animated short clips |
| **3D Modelling & Animation** | Blender, Maya, Low-poly art, Lighting & Texturing | 3D environment models, rendered product concepts |

---

## 🔒 Capacity Management Notice

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Due to high specialized software and workshop equipment requirements, the 2D & 3D Club operates with a strict membership registration cap. Once capacity is reached, the automated enrollment system conditionally branches new applicants to a waitlist or alternative club selections.
