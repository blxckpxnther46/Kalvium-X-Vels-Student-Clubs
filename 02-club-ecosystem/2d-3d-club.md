# 2D & 3D Club

The 2D & 3D Club focuses on visual creativity, digital art, UI design, animation, and 3D modeling.

## Key Activities

- Digital Art & Illustration: Concept art, character design, and digital painting.
- UI/UX Design: App interfaces, website layouts, and Figma prototypes.
- 2D & 3D Animation: Game sprites, motion graphics, and 3D environment assets.

Members work with tools like Figma, Blender, Unity, and Krita to produce visual deliverables for their portfolios.
