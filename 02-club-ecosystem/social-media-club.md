# Social Media Club

The Social Media Club focuses on content creation, video production, audio editing, and digital storytelling.

## Key Activities

- Video Editing & Filmmaking: Recaps, short videos, and promo clips.
- Podcasts & Audio: Recording, scripting, and editing student podcasts.
- Graphic Design: Poster design, social graphics, and branding assets.

Members produce media content that highlights student projects and campus activities.
