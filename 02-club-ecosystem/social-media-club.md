# Social Media Club

> **Core Action Tag**: `TELL / AMPLIFY`  
> **Primary Focus**: Content production, digital storytelling, media management, video editing, and public growth campaigns.

---

## 📢 Overview & Purpose

The **Social Media Club** is the digital mouthpiece and media engine of the student body. Members learn to craft compelling narratives, produce high-quality audio/video media, manage digital brand presences, and execute "Build in Public" growth strategies.

---

## 🎬 Key Activity Streams

- **Podcasts & Audio Production**: Scripting, recording, and editing student tech and career podcast episodes.
- **Filmmaking & Video Editing**: Short documentaries, event recap reels, promotional videos, and YouTube content.
- **Graphic Design & Poster Making**: Event posters, infographics, brand kits, and visual assets.
- **Media Management & Analytics**: Platform strategy, audience engagement analysis, and social media scheduling.
- **Build in Public Initiatives**: Documenting student projects and ecosystem milestones live across LinkedIn, YouTube, and Instagram.

---

## 💡 Example Sprint Deliverable
A 3-part video podcast series interviewing senior students on tech internship preparations, accompanied by promotional reels and LinkedIn carousels.
