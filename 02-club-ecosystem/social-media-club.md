# Social Media Club

The Social Media Club is the digital storytelling and media production engine of the student body. Members learn to plan, record, edit, and publish high-quality audio, visual, and written media.

## Focus Areas

The club focuses on content production, media, storytelling, documentation, digital communication, visual storytelling, media strategy, and platform growth.

## Activities & Exploration

Activities in the club can include:

- Audio Production & Podcasting: Scripting, recording, interviewing, and sound editing for student podcasts.
- Videography & Editing: Short video filmmaking, event recaps, promotional trailers, YouTube content editing, and color grading.
- Photography & Graphic Design: Event photography, poster making, infographics, visual social assets, and brand design.
- Media Strategy & Growth: Platform analytics, campaign development, content calendars, and Build in Public tracking.

These activities are examples of what the club can undertake. Members and leaders are free to experiment with new media formats, campaign ideas, and production styles.
