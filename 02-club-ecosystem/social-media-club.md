# Social Media Club

The Social Media Club is the digital storytelling and media production engine of the student body. Members learn to plan, record, edit, and publish high-quality audio and video content.

## Focus Areas

The club focuses on content production, storytelling, video editing, podcasting, graphic design, media strategy, and cross-platform growth campaigns.

## Activities & Projects

Members participate in media production activities, including:

- Podcast production, episode scripting, audio recording, and editing.
- Short video filmmaking, event recaps, promotional trailers, and YouTube content editing.
- Poster design, event graphics, infographics, and visual social assets.
- Media analytics, audience growth strategy, and Build in Public campaign tracking.

The club plays a major role in documenting student achievements and sharing project highlights across campus media channels.
