# Debate & Public Speaking Club

> **Core Action Tag**: `COMMUNICATE / LEAD`  
> **Primary Focus**: Public speaking, argumentation, persuasive presentation, rhetoric, confidence, and stage presence.

---

## 🎙️ Overview & Purpose

The **Debate & Public Speaking Club** develops confident articulators and persuasive communicators. Members master structured argumentation, impromptu speaking, elevator pitching, and formal keynote delivery, preparing them to command any stage or boardroom.

---

## 🗣️ Key Activity Streams

- **Structured Debates**: Parliamentary debates, Oxford-style debates, and policy discussions on tech and societal issues.
- **Group Discussions (GD)**: Industry-style GD simulations, consensus building, and leadership dynamics.
- **Pitching & Elevator Talks**: Product startup pitching, idea defense, and venture capital presentation roleplays.
- **Impromptu & JAM (Just a Minute)**: Rapid thinking exercises, fluency under pressure, and speech structure control.
- **TED-Style & Tech Talks**: Prepared keynote speeches, storytelling narratives, and visual slide integration.

---

## 📈 Monthly Sprint Outcome
Hosting a live campus **Keynote Competition** where participants pitch a simulated technology solution in 3 minutes to a panel of student and faculty judges.
