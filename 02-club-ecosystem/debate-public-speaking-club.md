# Debate & Public Speaking Club

The Debate & Public Speaking Club develops clear communication, persuasive argumentation, and public speaking confidence.

## Key Activities

- Structured Debates: Debating tech, business, and social topics.
- Pitching & Elevator Talks: Presenting ideas concisely and pitching simulated product ideas.
- Keynotes & Tech Talks: Delivering prepared talks using effective speech structures.

Members practice speaking under time constraints to improve stage presence and clarity.
