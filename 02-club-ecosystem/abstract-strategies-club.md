# Abstract Strategies Club

The Abstract Strategies Club focuses on strategic thinking, analytical decision-making, game theory, and practical strategy.

## Key Activities

- Strategy Games: Chess, Monopoly, and tactical board games.
- Business Strategy: Analyzing business models, marketing strategies, and market case studies.
- Ethical Negotiation: Scenario-based exercises focusing on communication, rapport building, and active listening.

HUMINT and negotiation exercises are strictly limited to ethical communication, active listening, and negotiation roleplay. Covert collection of personal information is prohibited.
