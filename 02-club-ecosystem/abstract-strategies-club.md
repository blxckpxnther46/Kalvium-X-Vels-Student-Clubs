# Abstract Strategies Club

> **Core Action Tag**: `THINK / DECIDE`  
> **Primary Focus**: Strategic thinking, analytical decision-making, game theory, practical business strategy, and negotiation.

---

## ♟️ Overview & Purpose

The **Abstract Strategies Club** focuses on sharpening mental agility, logical reasoning, and strategic foresight. Through competitive board games, real-world case tear-downs, and ethical negotiation simulations, members learn to navigate complex decision environments.

---

## 🧩 Key Activity Streams

| Activity Track | Focus Area | Core Methodologies / Formats |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Strategic Board Games** | Chess, Monopoly, Risk | Tournament play, opening theory analysis, endgame positioning |
| **Business & Market Strategy** | Corporate strategy, Go-To-Market | Business case studies, market teardowns, competitive analysis |
| **Ethical HUMINT & Negotiation** | Interpersonal observation, interviewing | Ethical rapport-building, negotiation roleplay, scenario exercises |
| **Financial & Life Strategies** | Resource allocation, personal finance | Risk management frameworks, decision trees, budgeting exercises |

---

## 🛡️ Ethical Guidelines for HUMINT & Negotiation Exercises

> [!IMPORTANT]
> All Human Intelligence (HUMINT) and negotiation activities are strictly framed around ethical communication skills: active listening, structured interviewing, non-verbal observation, rapport building, and scenario negotiation. **Under no circumstances** are activities conducted around covert surveillance or gathering private personal information.
