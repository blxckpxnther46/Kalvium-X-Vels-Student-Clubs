# Abstract Strategies Club

The Abstract Strategies Club sharpens analytical thinking, decision-making under uncertainty, and strategic foresight through competitive games, case analysis, and scenario exercises.

## Focus Areas

The club focuses on strategic thinking, decision-making, problem-solving, negotiation, logical reasoning, planning, and practical strategy.

## Activities & Exploration

Activities in the club can include:

- Tactical & Board Games: Chess, Monopoly, Risk, strategy game tournaments, and opening/endgame analysis.
- Business & Market Strategy: Corporate case study teardowns, market entry strategies, business model design, and competitive analysis.
- Ethical HUMINT & Negotiation: Scenario-based negotiation, active listening, rapport-building roleplay, interviewing techniques, and information assessment.
- Financial & Life Strategy: Personal resource allocation, budgeting models, risk assessment frameworks, and decision trees.

HUMINT activities are strictly framed around ethical communication, interviewing, observation, rapport-building, information assessment, negotiation, and scenario-based exercises. Covert gathering of personal information is prohibited.

These activities provide direction for the club, but members are encouraged to propose new strategy simulations, game formats, and analytical exercises.
