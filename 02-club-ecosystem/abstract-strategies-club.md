# Abstract Strategies Club

The Abstract Strategies Club sharpens analytical thinking, decision-making under uncertainty, and strategic foresight through competitive games and real-world case analysis.

## Focus Areas

The club focuses on strategic thinking, analytical problem-solving, game theory, practical business strategy, negotiation tactics, and financial reasoning.

## Activities & Projects

Members participate in analytical and strategic activities, including:

- Strategic board games, chess tournaments, Monopoly, and tactical gameplay analysis.
- Business case tear-downs, market analysis, and corporate strategy teardowns.
- Ethical negotiation roleplays, structured interviewing, active listening, and rapport-building scenarios.
- Financial decision modeling, risk management exercises, and personal resource strategy.

HUMINT-related activities are strictly framed around ethical communication, active listening, observation, information assessment, and negotiation roleplay. Covert gathering of personal information is prohibited.
