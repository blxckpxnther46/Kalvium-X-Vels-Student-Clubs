# Inter-Club Collaboration

Clubs can collaborate on joint initiatives that require multiple skills, such as a product launch simulation requiring UI design, video promo, business strategy, and a live pitch.

## Collaboration Rules

- Members stay enrolled in their home club and fulfill their primary roster commitments.
- Joint projects must attribute contributions clearly to all participating clubs and members.
- Collaborations must be planned during the monthly sprint planning phase with approval from club Presidents.
