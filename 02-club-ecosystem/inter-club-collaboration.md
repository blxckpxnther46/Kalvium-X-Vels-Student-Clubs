# Inter-Club Collaboration

While students belong to only one club at a time under the One-Club-Only policy, clubs are encouraged to collaborate on joint projects that require complementary skills.

## How Collaboration Works

A joint project allows different clubs to contribute their specialized skills to a single initiative. For example, a simulated product launch might combine:

- Abstract Strategies Club developing the business model and market analysis.
- 2D & 3D Club designing the product UI mockups and 3D visuals.
- Social Media Club producing the promo video and podcast announcement.
- Debate & Public Speaking Club delivering the live launch keynote and pitch.

## Collaboration Rules

Members involved in joint projects remain enrolled in and accountable to their home club roster.

All published deliverables from joint projects must explicitly credit every contributing club and member. Joint projects must be planned in advance and approved by the Presidents of all participating clubs.
