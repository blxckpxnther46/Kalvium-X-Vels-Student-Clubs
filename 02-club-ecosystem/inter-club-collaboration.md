# Inter-Club Collaboration

While students belong to only one club at a time under the One-Club-Only policy, clubs are encouraged to collaborate on joint initiatives that require complementary skills.

## How Collaboration Works

A joint project or event allows different clubs to contribute their specialized skills to a single initiative. For example, a joint startup simulation might combine:

- Abstract Strategies Club developing the business strategy and market model.
- 2D & 3D Club designing the product UI mockups and visual branding.
- Social Media Club producing the promotional video trailer and podcast announcement.
- Debate & Public Speaking Club delivering the live pitch presentation to judges.

## Collaboration Rules

Members involved in joint projects remain enrolled in and accountable to their home club roster.

All published deliverables from joint projects must explicitly credit every contributing club and member. Joint initiatives should be planned in advance with approval from the Presidents of all participating clubs.
