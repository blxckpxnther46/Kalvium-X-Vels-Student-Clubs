# Campus & Management Coordination

Smooth integration with university infrastructure is essential for the ecosystem's success. The Operations Team acts as the official bridge between student clubs and university administration.

---

## 🏫 Key Areas of Campus Interface

1. **Venue & Lab Allocation**: Securing computer labs, seminar halls, auditoriums, and open spaces for Growth Hours and Showcase Days.
2. **Timetable & Academic Alignment**: Ensuring club activities do not conflict with examinations, official academic sessions, or university holidays.
3. **Equipment & AV Support**: Coordinating requests for projectors, microphones, podcast recording apparatus, and high-performance workstation access.
4. **Official Permissions**: Obtaining requisite approvals for campus visitors, guest speakers, or public media broadcasts.

---

## 📝 Communication Protocol
All official communications addressed to university deans, department heads, or facility managers must be routed through or cleared by the Operations Team to maintain professional consistency.
