# Decision-Making Framework

To maintain operational speed while respecting governance boundaries, decisions within the ecosystem are categorized into three clear levels:

---

## 🚦 Decision Matrix

| Level | Decision Scope | Approval Authority | Examples |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Level 1: Operational** | Day-to-day club activities, session agendas, internal task assignments | Club President & Vice President | Selecting a weekly workshop topic, assigning presentation roles |
| **Level 2: Ecosystem** | Roster transfers, inter-club events, schedule changes, resource distribution | Operations Team + Affected Club Leaders | Approving a cross-club project, resolving a transfer request |
| **Level 3: Governance** | Policy edits, handbook amendments, campus escalation, leadership removal | Operations Team + Faculty Mentors + Campus Admin | Modifying the Code of Conduct, approving new club proposals |

---

## ⏱️ Resolution Workflows
- **Standard Proposals**: Submitted 5 business days prior to scheduled execution.
- **Urgent Logistics**: Resolved within 24 hours via direct Operations Team sync.
