# Student Club Operations Team

The Kalvium x Vels Student Club Operations Team coordinates the overall ecosystem, works with campus management and mentors, and supports club leadership with scheduling, enrollment, and shared processes.

The Operations Team establishes the common framework that applies across all clubs. It does not dictate internal club activities, workshop topics, or project choices, which remain under the direction of elected club leaders and active members.
