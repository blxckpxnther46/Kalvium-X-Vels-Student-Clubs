# Student Club Operations Team

The overall governing and coordinating body of the ecosystem is the **Kalvium × Vels Student Club Operations Team**.

---

## 🎯 Core Scope & Responsibilities

The Operations Team handles ecosystem-wide administrative and logistical functions:

1. **Ecosystem Maintenance**: Maintaining overall governance policies, handbook documentation, and brand standards.
2. **Campus Liaison**: Coordinating room allocations, equipment access, and schedule alignments with campus administration.
3. **Mentor Coordination**: Facilitating faculty mentor onboarding, meeting schedules, and milestone reviews.
4. **Enrollment & Roster Management**: Overseeing the central enrollment portal, conditional branching, and capacity caps.
5. **Elections & Succession**: Supporting transparent leadership elections and transition handovers across cycles.
6. **Inter-Club Synergy**: Facilitating cross-club collaboration approvals and joint event logistics.

---

## ⚠️ Distinction from Club Leadership

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The Operations Team provides overarching administrative support and system governance. **It does NOT replace the elected leadership of individual clubs.** Day-to-day club sessions, project sprints, and internal club agendas remain under the direct leadership of each club's elected President and Vice President.
