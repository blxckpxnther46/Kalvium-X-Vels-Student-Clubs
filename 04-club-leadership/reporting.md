# Weekly & Monthly Reports

Leadership reporting provides visibility into club progress and enables mentors to offer timely support without creating unnecessary bureaucracy.

## Weekly Report

Each club leadership team provides a concise weekly report covering the relevant activity of that week.

The weekly report captures:

- Activities or events conducted during the week.
- Member participation and engagement.
- Progress on ongoing work or projects.
- Important outcomes or milestones.
- Problems, blockers, or support required.
- Plans for the upcoming week.

Weekly reports reflect whatever the club actually did that week, whether that was an event, workshop, project build, learning session, discussion, or challenge.

## Monthly Report

The monthly report provides a broader view of the entire cycle and functions as a retrospective.

The monthly report covers:

- Major activities and events conducted.
- Projects or creative work completed.
- Individual and collaborative learning achievements.
- Member participation summary.
- Showcase Day output and reception.
- Challenges encountered and lessons learned.
- Suggestions and plans for the next cycle.

## Mentor Review

Faculty mentors collect and review weekly and monthly reports. The reports give mentors clear visibility into club progress, allowing them to provide guidance, resources, and administrative support where needed.
