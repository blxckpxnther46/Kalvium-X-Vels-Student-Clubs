# Leadership Deliverables

To maintain operational quality and accountability, student leaders must submit standard deliverables throughout each cycle.

---

## 📦 Mandatory Officer Deliverables

| Deliverable | Responsible Officer | Frequency / Deadline | Target Audience |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Monthly Sprint Plan** | President | 3 days before cycle kickoff | Members, Ops Team, Mentor |
| **Weekly Attendance Log** | Vice President | Within 24 hrs of session | Operations Team |
| **Showcase Submission Package** | President & VP | 24 hrs before Showcase Day | Showcase Judges & Campus |
| **Cycle Retrospective Report** | President | 2 days post-Showcase Day | Operations Team & Mentor |

---

## 📁 Storage & Submission
All deliverables must be formatted according to official templates and uploaded to the designated [Documentation Archive](../09-documentation/README.md).
