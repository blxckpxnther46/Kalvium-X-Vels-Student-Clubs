# Campus Coordination & Enrollment

The Operations Team serves as a connection between the student-club ecosystem and the campus. It helps coordinate club requirements with the appropriate faculty or campus channels when required, while bringing relevant campus information back to the clubs.

## Facility & Calendar Alignment

Club leadership identifies the space, timing, and permission requirements for their activities. The Operations Team helps coordinate with the relevant faculty or campus authority when permission or coordination is required.

Coordination support includes:

- Coordinating access to suitable spaces when required.
- Requesting or facilitating faculty permission for club activities or events.
- Helping align club activities with the academic calendar, exams, and university holidays.

Club leadership owns the activity. Operations helps connect the activity with the appropriate campus or faculty coordination when required. The Operations Team does not provide or guarantee equipment or specific facilities, and normal club activities do not require separate Operations approval.

## Enrollment & Central Rosters

The Operations Team maintains the central roster of active members across all four clubs. Student enrollment is processed through an official registration form maintained by Operations.

Vice Presidents of individual clubs submit verified attendance and roster updates at the end of each cycle. This ensures accurate records across the ecosystem and supports smooth member transitions during club switching windows.
