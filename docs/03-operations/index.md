# Student Club Operations Team

The Kalvium x Vels Student Club Operations Team coordinates the overall ecosystem, works with campus management and mentors, and supports club leadership with scheduling, enrollment, and shared processes.

The Operations Team establishes the common framework that applies across all clubs. It does not dictate internal club activities, workshop topics, or project choices, which remain under the direction of elected club leaders and active members.

## Operations Coordinators

Founding members of the central team use the designation Founding Member & Operations Coordinator. This designation is equal among all founding operations team members. Operations Coordinators act as operational partners and facilitators for elected Club Presidents.

## Decision-Making Framework

- Framework Level Decisions: Handled by the Operations Team, including overall registration windows, schedule alignment with campus management, facility requests, roster management, and governance guidelines.
- Club Level Decisions: Handled by individual club leadership teams, including weekly session topics, event planning, project selection, internal task assignments, Showcase Day preparation, and club culture.
