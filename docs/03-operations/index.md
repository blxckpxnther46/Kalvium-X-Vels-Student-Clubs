# Student Club Operations Team

The Operations Team took the initiative to establish the Student Clubs ecosystem and continues to maintain the common framework that allows clubs to operate consistently and stay connected with the campus.

The Operations Team is a coordination layer within the student-club ecosystem, not a management layer above it.

## Ecosystem Roles

The Student Clubs ecosystem functions through three complementary roles:

- **Operations Team:** Establishes, coordinates, connects, and maintains continuity across the student-club ecosystem.
- **Club Leadership:** Runs, plans, and develops individual clubs.
- **Members:** Participate, contribute, create, learn, and collaborate.

## Founding Role & Continuity

The founding role of the Operations Team includes establishing the common Student Clubs framework, creating shared operating principles, coordinating across clubs, maintaining system continuity, facilitating campus alignment, and initiating overall ecosystem improvements.

The Operations Team established and maintains the ecosystem, while individual Club Leadership runs their own clubs. Being a founding member does not confer permanent authority over clubs, the authority to override Club Presidents, ownership of individual club activities, or control over club creative direction.

## Operations Coordinators as Active Members

Every member of the Operations Team is also an active member of one of the Student Clubs. Being an Operations Coordinator is an additional responsibility alongside their club membership, rather than a separate position outside the clubs.

## Scope of Responsibility

The Operations Team provides ecosystem coordination rather than creative control over individual clubs.

### Operations Responsibilities

- Maintaining the common Student Clubs framework.
- Coordinating shared structures and schedules such as Growth Hours.
- Maintaining continuity across monthly cycles and leadership transitions.
- Coordinating with faculty and campus channels when required.
- Helping clubs navigate campus-level coordination.
- Supporting cross-club activities and joint initiatives.
- Maintaining common documentation and institutional records.
- Facilitating resolution for issues that involve multiple clubs.
- Initiating improvements to the overall Student Clubs system.

### Club Leadership Autonomy

Club Presidents and their leadership teams independently direct their clubs. Operations does not:

- Direct individual club strategy or workshop topics.
- Approve ordinary club activities or session plans.
- Control Club Presidents or member project choices.
- Run daily club sessions or dictate creative direction.
- Provide, supply, or guarantee equipment or specific facilities.

Club Leaders can and should handle normal responsibilities for their own clubs. Operations exists primarily where coordination extends beyond an individual club.
