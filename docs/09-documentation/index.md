# Project Documentation & Archives

Clubs maintain practical documentation to preserve work, ensure continuity for future leadership, and celebrate member achievements.

## Project Repositories & Build Logs

Completed club projects are saved in designated code or design repositories. Each project includes a brief summary detailing team members, tools used, and links or screenshots demonstrating the final work.

## Meeting Records & Attendance

Vice Presidents maintain session attendance logs and keep brief notes of leadership planning meetings to ensure operational clarity and continuity for future leadership teams.

## Showcase Archive & Wall of Fame

The Showcase Archive holds completed project outputs and event records from previous monthly cycles. Members can review past entries for inspiration and track skill progression over time. Outstanding work and contributions presented on Showcase Day may be highlighted on the campus Wall of Fame or shared across university media channels to recognize student effort.
