# Growth Hours & Club Activities

The 4:00 PM to 6:00 PM period is referred to as Growth Hours because club activities contribute directly to student growth pillars.

## Proposed Schedule

- Tuesday: 4:00 PM to 6:00 PM
- Thursday: 4:00 PM to 6:00 PM
- Saturday: Available hours on working Saturdays, excluding scheduled classes.

These timings are proposed and subject to final campus approval.

The proposed schedule may be adjusted to accommodate academic and institutional commitments. Alternative arrangements, including suitable portions of the Work Integration period, may be considered where feasible and subject to approval.

## Session Cadence & Flexibility

Growth Hours sessions run for 2 hours. Club leaders are free to design session structures that suit their planned activities, whether that involves a technical workshop, debate practice, gameplay analysis, project work, or media recording.

Sessions typically balance brief briefings, core activity execution, hands-on practice, and wrap-up logging.
