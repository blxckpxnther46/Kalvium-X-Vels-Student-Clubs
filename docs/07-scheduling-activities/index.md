# Club Hours & Activity Scheduling

Kalvium provides designated time periods known as Growth Hours to support overall student development. The Student Clubs ecosystem utilizes the available Growth Hours as Club Hours for club activities and collaboration.

## Relationship Between Growth Hours and Club Hours

Growth Hours represent the broader time allocation provided by Kalvium for student growth. Club Hours refer specifically to the operating time within those available hours allocated to Student Club activities.

![Growth Hours & Club Hours Hierarchy Flowchart](/img/diagrams/club-hours.jpg)

## Club Hours Schedule

Student Club activities operate during the following designated Club Hours:

- **Tuesday:** 4:00 PM to 6:00 PM
- **Thursday:** 4:00 PM to 6:00 PM
- **Saturday:** When Saturday is a working day, available Growth Hours may be used as Club Hours, excluding scheduled academic sessions.
- **Other Available Periods:** Any available Growth Hours not occupied by scheduled academic sessions may also be used as Club Hours.

## Academic Session Priority

Scheduled academic sessions remain separate and take precedence over Club Hours. Club Hours apply to available time outside scheduled academic sessions. If an available Growth Hour conflicts with a scheduled academic session, the academic session takes precedence.

## Room Allocation & Physical Seating

During Club Hours, students move from their usual seating arrangement to the room assigned to their respective club.

Club Hours are therefore the designated time when students physically operate from their respective club spaces, work alongside peers in the same club, and participate in club activities.

## Session Flexibility & Cadence

Club Hours sessions typically run for 2 hours. Club leaders design session structures that suit their planned activities, whether that involves a workshop, debate practice, gameplay analysis, project build, or media recording.
