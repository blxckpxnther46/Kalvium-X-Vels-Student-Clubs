# Joining & Member Expectations

Students join a club by completing the official registration form during published enrollment windows.

## Registration Process

To join, students submit their details and select one club for the active cycle. Enrollment may be subject to available capacity. Once registered, members receive session location details and welcome information from their club Vice President.

## Member Responsibilities

Active membership requires regular participation and constructive teamwork. Members are expected to attend Growth Hours sessions, contribute to their club's activities or projects, respect peers and equipment, and follow the Code of Conduct. Members who miss multiple sessions without notice may lose active member status for that cycle.

## Leadership Pathway

Every active member has the opportunity to advance into leadership roles. Members who demonstrate initiative, reliable participation, and strong collaboration during regular sessions can apply for Club Coordinator positions. Experienced coordinators can progress to Vice President and President roles during annual selection windows.
