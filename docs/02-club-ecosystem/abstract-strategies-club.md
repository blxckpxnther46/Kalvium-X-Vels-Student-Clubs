# Abstract Strategies Club

### About the Club

The Abstract Strategies Club sharpens analytical reasoning, tactical thinking, and strategic decision-making under uncertainty. It serves students interested in competitive board games, corporate strategy, game theory, ethical negotiation, and financial decision modeling.

### Areas of Exploration

Members can explore strategic disciplines, including:

- Tactical & Board Games: Chess opening theory, endgame tactics, Monopoly resource trading, and Risk positioning.
- Business & Market Strategy: Corporate case study teardowns, market entry strategies, business model evaluation, and competitive positioning.
- Ethical HUMINT & Negotiation: Scenario-based negotiation roleplay, active listening, structured interviewing, rapport building, and information assessment.
- Financial Strategy: Risk management modeling, personal budgeting, resource allocation, and decision-tree construction.

### What Members Can Explore

Students can practice evaluating complex trade-offs, analyzing competitive moves, structuring strategic business teardowns, and conducting ethical negotiations. Members learn how strategic frameworks apply to both game boards and professional decision-making.

### Activities & Experiences

Activities in the club include:

- Chess tournaments and tactical endgame analysis sessions.
- Corporate strategy case teardowns and business model challenges.
- Scenario-based negotiation roleplay and active listening practice.
- Risk modeling challenges and strategic decision simulations.

HUMINT activities are strictly framed around ethical communication, interviewing, observation, rapport-building, information assessment, negotiation, and scenario-based exercises. Covert gathering of personal information is prohibited.

### Projects & Creative Work

Examples of possible outputs include:

- A documented business strategy teardown analyzing a modern tech company's market position.
- A strategic decision framework and risk management model for project planning.
- A recorded negotiation scenario simulation demonstrating effective rapport-building techniques.
- A competitive chess tournament log detailing opening variations and strategic key moves.

These examples provide direction for the club, but members are encouraged to propose new strategy simulations, game formats, and analytical exercises.

### Events & Showcase

The Abstract Strategies Club presents its work on Showcase Day through live strategy tournament finals, interactive business teardown keynotes, negotiation demonstration roleplays, and decision modeling presentations.
