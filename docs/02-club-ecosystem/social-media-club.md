# Social Media Club

### About the Club

The Social Media Club is the digital storytelling and media production engine of the student body. It serves students interested in content creation, video production, audio engineering, graphic design, and brand management. The club provides a space to turn creative narrative ideas into published media artifacts.

### Areas of Exploration

Members can explore various media production fields, including:

- Content Creation & Podcasting: Podcast recording, interviewing, audio editing, and sound design.
- Videography & Editing: Short film production, event recap reels, promotional video editing, and color grading.
- Photography & Graphic Design: Event photography, poster making, infographics, social media carousels, and visual branding.
- Media Strategy & Build in Public: Audience analytics, content calendars, platform strategy, and campaign tracking.

### What Members Can Explore

Students can learn how to write compelling scripts, operate camera and audio equipment, edit videos, design promotional posters, and analyze viewer engagement. Members can experiment with digital storytelling formats across platforms like YouTube, Instagram, and LinkedIn.

### Activities & Experiences

Activities in the club include:

- Hands-on video editing and podcast recording sessions.
- Film recaps, photography walks, and poster design challenges.
- Build in Public initiatives documenting student achievements across campus.
- Collaborative media campaigns supporting campus events and joint club launches.

### Projects & Creative Work

Examples of possible outputs include:

- A multi-episode video podcast series interviewing student developers and founders.
- A polished promotional mini-documentary or event recap reel.
- A complete graphic design kit and poster campaign for a campus initiative.
- A documented Build in Public campaign tracking a multi-week project rollout.

These project ideas serve as inspiration, not boundaries. Members are free to experiment with new content formats, video styles, and media strategies.

### Events & Showcase

The Social Media Club showcases its work on Showcase Day through video screenings, live podcast launches, photo galleries, and campaign analytics breakdowns. The club can also host short film screenings, media production workshops, and poster competitions.
