# 2D & 3D Club

### About the Club

The 2D & 3D Club is the visual design and creative hub of the ecosystem. It brings together digital artists, UI designers, animators, and 3D modelers to turn creative concepts into visual assets, interactive prototypes, and digital artwork. The club serves students who have a passion for visual aesthetics, design systems, digital illustration, and game development.

### Areas of Exploration

Members can explore a wide spectrum of visual design domains, including:

- Painting & Drawing: Concept art, character design, digital painting, and sketching.
- UI/UX Design: User interface design, mobile app wireframing, website mockups, and Figma design systems.
- 2D Animation & Game Dev: Character sprite design, 2D motion graphics, indie game prototypes, and Unity or Godot scripting.
- 3D Modeling & Rendering: Low-poly art, 3D asset creation, environment modeling, texturing, lighting, and Blender rendering.

### What Members Can Explore

Students can learn fundamental and advanced design techniques, practice digital illustration, experiment with interactive UI components, and build 3D game models. Members can explore how visual design connects with software development, user experience, and interactive storytelling.

### Activities & Experiences

Activities in the club combine structured learning with creative freedom, including:

- Hands-on workshops on software tools like Figma, Blender, and Unity.
- Creative design challenges, speed painting sessions, and 3D asset modeling sprints.
- Collaborative game development sessions and peer code/design reviews.
- Portfolio review sessions where senior members share feedback on visual techniques.

### Projects & Creative Work

Examples of possible outputs include:

- A complete UI/UX mobile application prototype designed in Figma.
- A 2D indie game demo with custom animated character sprites.
- A collection of rendered 3D low-poly environment assets and models.
- Digital art concept sheets and character illustrations.

These examples illustrate what members can produce, but they are not limitations. Members and leaders are encouraged to propose new visual experiments and creative formats.

### Events & Showcase

The 2D & 3D Club presents its achievements during monthly Showcase Days through interactive art galleries, UI prototype demonstrations, live game demos, and 3D render displays. The club can also host design battles, game jams, and digital art exhibitions.
