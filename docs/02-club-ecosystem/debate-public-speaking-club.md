# Debate & Public Speaking Club

### About the Club

The Debate & Public Speaking Club develops confident articulators, logical debaters, and persuasive public speakers. It serves students who want to improve their stage presence, speech structuring, argumentation, impromptu fluency, and elevator pitch delivery.

### Areas of Exploration

Members can explore public communication formats, including:

- Structured Debates: Parliamentary debates, Oxford-style debates, and policy discussions on tech and social issues.
- Group Discussions: Consensus building, group dynamics, moderation, and discussion leadership.
- Pitching & Elevator Talks: Startup pitch presentations, elevator speeches, and idea defense roleplay.
- Impromptu Speaking & JAM: Rapid thinking exercises, Just a Minute (JAM) sessions, and speech fluency under time limits.
- Keynote Presentations: Story-driven talks, TED-style keynotes, and persuasive slide presentations.

### What Members Can Explore

Students can learn how to structure persuasive arguments, overcome stage anxiety, use vocal modulation effectively, deliver concise elevator pitches, and construct logical speech outlines.

### Activities & Experiences

Activities in the club include:

- Weekly debate rounds and group discussion simulations.
- Impromptu JAM challenges and elevator pitch battles.
- Keynote speech preparation and peer feedback sessions.
- Rhetoric workshops analyzing famous historical speeches and modern TED keynotes.

### Projects & Creative Work

Examples of possible outputs include:

- A recorded 3-minute TED-style keynote speech on a technology innovation.
- A documented parliamentary debate transcript analyzing a major tech policy topic.
- A polished startup pitch presentation deck and live defense recording.
- A series of JAM speech exercises demonstrating speech fluency and structure.

These examples serve to inspire session ideas. Leaders and members can introduce new debate formats, speaking challenges, and rhetoric workshops.

### Events & Showcase

The Debate & Public Speaking Club contributes to Showcase Day by hosting live debate finals, pitch competition showcases, keynote speech sessions, and impromptu speaking demonstrations for the campus audience.
