# Leadership Operating Model

While club leadership has full freedom over creative and activity choices, all leaders follow a common operating model to guide their club through each cycle:

Understand -> Plan -> Execute -> Build -> Showcase -> Reflect

```text
[ Understand ] ──► [ Plan ] ──► [ Execute ] ──► [ Build ] ──► [ Showcase ] ──► [ Reflect ]
```

## Understand

Leadership begins by understanding the club members: their interests, existing skills, ideas, availability, and the direction they want the club to take.

## Plan

The leadership team decides what the club will do during the cycle. This may include events, workshops, projects, discussions, challenges, learning sessions, collaborations, competitions, or other activities. There is no requirement to use a specific combination; leadership decides based on the club's identity and member input.

## Execute

Leadership coordinates the activities and ensures members have meaningful opportunities to participate. The President and Vice President enable members to take ownership rather than doing everything themselves.

## Build

Where appropriate, members create projects, outputs, demonstrations, or event artifacts that represent their learning. Building does not necessarily mean software; it can be artwork, media content, strategy models, or speech performances.

## Showcase

Leadership organizes the club's Showcase Day contribution and ensures the work accomplished during the cycle is properly presented.

## Reflect

After the cycle, leadership reviews what worked, what could improve, what members enjoyed, what members learned, and what should change in the next cycle.
