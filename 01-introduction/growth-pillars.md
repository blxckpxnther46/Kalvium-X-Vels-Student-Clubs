# Student Growth Pillars

The Student Club ecosystem activities directly map to four core **Student Growth Pillars**. These pillars ensure that club participation contributes meaningfully to students' overall career readiness.

---

## 📊 Growth Pillars Mapping Matrix

| Growth Pillar | Primary Club Alignment | Target Competencies | Key Outputs |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Visual & Technical Creativity** | 2D & 3D Club | UI/UX, 3D Modelling, 2D Animation, Game Assets, Digital Rendering | Game prototypes, UI mockups, 3D assets |
| **2. Media & Digital Communication** | Social Media Club | Content creation, Video editing, Podcasting, Brand positioning, Public growth | Tech podcasts, video campaigns, social posters |
| **3. Strategic & Analytical Thinking** | Abstract Strategies Club | Business strategy, Game theory, Negotiation tactics, Financial modeling | Case study reports, strategy tear-downs, chess logs |
| **4. Rhetoric & Leadership** | Debate & Public Speaking | Argumentation, Impromptu speaking, Pitching, Stage presence, Event hosting | Keynote talks, debate wins, elevator pitches |

---

## ⏱️ Alignment with Growth Hours

Activities within these pillars take place during dedicated **Growth Hours** (typically 4:00 PM – 6:00 PM on Tuesdays and Thursdays). This ensures structured, distraction-free time dedicated exclusively to portfolio and skill building.
