# Introduction

The Kalvium x Vels Student Clubs ecosystem was created to give students dedicated time and space to develop practical skills outside traditional coursework. Through peer collaboration, project sprints, and monthly showcases, students build tangible artifacts while developing leadership and teamwork experience.

The ecosystem balances central coordination with club autonomy. The central framework ensures consistency in scheduling, membership rules, and showcase opportunities, while individual club leadership teams design their own activities and projects.
