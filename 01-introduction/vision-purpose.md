# Vision & Purpose

The purpose of the Student Clubs is to help students build real-world capabilities through continuous hands-on creation and peer learning.

## Practical Skill Development

Rather than sitting through passive presentations, club members spend session time actively building, writing, analyzing, debating, or designing. This approach ensures that members leave each monthly cycle with completed project artifacts.

## Portfolio & Career Readiness

The outputs created during club sessions serve as concrete evidence of your skills. Completed game prototypes, UI kits, media campaigns, case analyses, and recorded keynotes contribute directly to student portfolios for future internships and career opportunities.
