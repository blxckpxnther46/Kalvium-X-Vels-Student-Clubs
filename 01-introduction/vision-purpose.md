# Vision & Purpose

The purpose of the Student Clubs is to help students build real-world skills through hands-on projects, teamwork, and regular showcases.

## Core Objectives

- Practical Learning: Apply design, media, strategy, and speaking skills in real projects.
- Peer Mentorship: Work across academic years to share knowledge and technical tips.
- Portfolio Building: Produce completed work that can be demonstrated during monthly showcases and added to student portfolios.
