# Welcome to Student Clubs

The Student Clubs provide a space where you can turn ideas into real projects, learn skills from your peers, and build a strong portfolio. Whether you want to design 3D game assets, produce podcasts, analyze market strategies, or command a stage during public debates, there is a community here for you.

Clubs are run by students for students. Faculty mentors provide advice and guidance, but day-to-day session planning, project ideas, and team organization are led by elected student officers.

## How to Get Started

Explore the active clubs in the [Club Ecosystem](../02-club-ecosystem/README.md) section to find the area that aligns best with your goals. When you are ready to join, review [Joining a Club](../05-membership/joining.md) for enrollment instructions.
