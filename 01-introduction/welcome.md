# Welcome to Student Clubs

Welcome to the **Kalvium × Vels Student Clubs**! 

This ecosystem was established to empower students beyond the traditional academic curriculum. Whether you want to master 3D game animation, host a podcast, analyze strategic market moves, or deliver powerful public speeches, these clubs provide the platform, community, and structured time to bring your ideas to life.

---

## 🎯 What Makes Us Different?

1. **Student-Driven Execution**: Clubs are led by elected student officers with guidance from dedicated faculty mentors.
2. **Action Over Passive Attendance**: Members do not just sit in lectures—they build, present, debate, and publish real artifacts.
3. **Structured Growth Cadence**: Monthly cycles allow members to work on focused project sprints culminating in a campus-wide **Showcase Day**.
4. **Interdisciplinary Skill Building**: Learn how design, media, strategy, and communication intersect in real-world scenarios.

---

## 🧭 How to Get Started

- Read about the [Four Active Clubs](../02-club-ecosystem/README.md) to find where your primary interest lies.
- Understand the [One-Club-Only Policy](../05-membership/one-club-rule.md) and how [Club Cycles](../05-membership/club-cycles.md) work.
- Complete your enrollment during active registration windows as detailed in [Joining a Club](../05-membership/joining.md).

We look forward to seeing your contributions across this academic year!
