# Welcome to Student Clubs

The Kalvium x Vels Student Clubs provide a structured space for students to explore interests, learn from peers, and build practical projects outside class hours.

Clubs are led by students, guided by faculty mentors, and focused on active participation rather than passive attendance.

## How to Get Started

Read about the four active clubs in the [Club Ecosystem](../02-club-ecosystem/README.md) section. Once you choose a club, check [Joining a Club](../05-membership/joining.md) to complete your registration.
