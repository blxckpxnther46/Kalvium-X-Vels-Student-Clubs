# Useful Tools & Directories

A curated list of software tools, learning platforms, and reference directories recommended for club members.

---

## 🛠️ Recommended Software per Discipline

- **2D & 3D Club**: [Figma](https://figma.com), [Blender](https://blender.org), [Unity](https://unity.com), [Krita](https://krita.org).
- **Social Media Club**: [CapCut](https://capcut.com), [DaVinci Resolve](https://blackmagicdesign.com), [Audacity](https://audacityteam.org), [Canva](https://canva.com).
- **Abstract Strategies Club**: [Chess.com](https://chess.com), [Lichess](https://lichess.org), [Harvard Business Review Cases](https://hbr.org).
- **Debate & Public Speaking**: [TED Talks Archive](https://ted.com), [Toastmasters International Resources](https://toastmasters.org).
