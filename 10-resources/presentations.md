# Slide Decks & Presentation Templates

To maintain visual cohesion during Showcase Days and campus events, all presentations must use official deck templates.

---

## 🎨 Slide Deck Assets

- **Official Showcase Master Deck (.pptx / Google Slides)**: Includes pre-formatted title slides, speaker profiles, project demo layouts, and closing slides.
- **Keynote Pitch Template**: Optimized for 3-minute rapid elevator pitches in Debate & Public Speaking Club.
- **Technical Teardown Template**: Designed for case study presentations in Abstract Strategies Club.
