# Forms, Templates & Resources

This section provides access to registration forms, transfer applications, slide templates, brand assets, and reference links.

## Official Forms & Dashboards

Official registration forms and roster documents are distributed by the Operations Team during open registration and transfer windows.

## Club Switching Applications

Transfer applications require basic information: student ID, current club, confirmation of showcase completion, and target club selection. Transfers are processed during the official window between Showcase Day and the start of the next cycle.

## Slide Decks & Brand Assets

Clubs use standardized slide deck templates during Showcase Day presentations to keep project demonstrations clear and visual. Official club logos and design assets are available for poster creation, slide decks, and media projects. Maintain standard colors and typography when using official logos.

## Recommended Tools Directory

Recommended tools for each club:

- 2D & 3D Club: Figma, Blender, Unity, Krita.
- Social Media Club: CapCut, DaVinci Resolve, Audacity, Canva.
- Abstract Strategies Club: Chess.com, Lichess, Harvard Business Review case studies.
- Debate & Public Speaking Club: TED Talks, Toastmasters speech archives.
