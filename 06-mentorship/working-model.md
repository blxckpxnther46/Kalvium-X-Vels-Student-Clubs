# Student–Mentor Working Model

The student–mentor relationship is structured around mutual respect, transparency, and regular check-in cadences.

---

## 📅 Interaction Cadence

| Interaction Event | Frequency | Primary Participants | Agenda / Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Monthly Sprint Briefing** | 1st week of cycle | Mentor + President & VP | Review proposed sprint goals & resource needs |
| **Mid-Cycle Check-in** | 3rd week of cycle | Mentor + Club Executive Board | Review build progress & address technical roadblocks |
| **Pre-Showcase Validation** | 2 days before Showcase | Mentor + Project Teams | Dry-run showcase presentations & give feedback |
| **Monthly Retrospective** | Post-Showcase Day | Mentor + Operations Team | Evaluate overall cycle outcomes & operational health |

---

## 🛠️ Best Practices for Student Officers
- Respect mentors' academic schedules by booking check-ins at least 48 hours in advance.
- Present clear agendas and progress summaries during every check-in meeting.
