# Mentor Responsibilities

While student officers drive execution, Club Mentors fulfill critical advisory and governance functions.

---

## 📜 Core Mentor Responsibilities

1. **Sprint Roadmap Validation**: Reviewing and providing constructive feedback on monthly club sprint proposals.
2. **Technical & Domain Guidance**: Recommending industry-standard tools, frameworks, learning resources, and methodologies.
3. **Milestone Reviews**: Attending monthly formal check-ins to review project progress prior to Showcase Day.
4. **Showcase Judging**: Serving as panel evaluators or identifying external guest judges for Showcase Day presentations.
5. **Institutional Support**: Assisting student leaders with formal campus permissions or facility access when required.
6. **Conflict Resolution**: Providing impartial advice if operational disputes arise within club executive boards.
