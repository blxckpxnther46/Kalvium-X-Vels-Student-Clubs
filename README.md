# Kalvium x Vels Student Clubs Handbook

The Kalvium x Vels Student Clubs ecosystem is a student-driven initiative designed to help students explore interests, build practical skills, collaborate with peers, and take ownership of projects beyond the academic curriculum.

This handbook serves as the single source of truth for students, elected club leaders, faculty mentors, and campus management. It outlines the common framework that keeps the ecosystem running smoothly while empowering individual clubs to build their own unique culture and activities.

## Core Philosophy

The central philosophy of this documentation and ecosystem is simple:

The Operations Team defines the common framework. Club leadership defines how their club operates within that framework.

The central framework establishes shared rules, such as enrollment windows, the One-Club-Only policy, the monthly Club Cycle, Showcase Day, and Growth Hours. Within those boundaries, each club leadership team has full creative freedom to decide its own projects, workshops, discussions, and internal culture.

## The Active Clubs

The ecosystem consists of four active clubs:

- 2D & 3D Club: Focused on visual creativity, digital art, UI design, 2D animation, game development, and 3D modeling.
- Social Media Club: Focused on content production, storytelling, podcasting, video editing, media management, and Build in Public campaigns.
- Abstract Strategies Club: Focused on strategic thinking, game theory, business strategy, negotiation tactics, and financial reasoning.
- Debate & Public Speaking Club: Focused on communication, public speaking, structured argumentation, idea pitching, and stage presence.
